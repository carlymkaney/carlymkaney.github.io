Membership Terms & Conditions

Sol Yoga offers two types of memberships:

Monthly Recurring Memberships

By signing up for this membership, you agree to pay $79 per month for at least three months via automatic payment on a credit card or withdrawal from a bank account you are also agreeing to the following terms:

  • After the minimum three months commitment is reached, this membership will automatically renew on a month-to-month basis until either you cancel your membership online via email to hello@solyoga.com with at least 10 days notice before your next renewal date.
  • Memberships cannot be suspended, placed on hold for travel reasons, or for personal reasons. Memberships may be paused in the event of a medical condition with a doctor’s note.
  • You may not purchase and schedule a membership to begin in the future. Upon purchasing the first month is immediately charged to your credit card on the day you sign up. Your monthly payments then run on the same day of the month as your initial purchase date (aka your renewal date).
  • We reserve the right to change the monthly price of this membership subscription anytime after your initial four month commitment is reached.

Class Pass Memberships

  • A class membership allows you to purchase and prepay for 10 or 20 classes. All class memberships expire after 365 days. You will lose any classes that were not taken during the 365 days membership term. No refund or credit will be given.
  • 10 and 20 class memberships can be shared with family and friends.

Sol Yoga LLC reserves the right to terminate a membership at anytime.
