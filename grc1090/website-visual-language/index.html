Our Philosophy

Sol Yoga is a special third place for all to feel welcome and inspired and safe while diving deeper into the practice of yoga. We are a community that supports each other in their own journey and invites everyone to show up as they are. With a wide variety of class options, Sol Yoga welcomes folks from all walks of life who are looking to deepen their relationship with yoga or who are just getting started. We believe that now is the time to create healthy change. Come join us at Sol Yoga where we are committed to creating a space where people feel welcomed, supported and able to experience the true benefits of yoga… body, mind, and Sol!
